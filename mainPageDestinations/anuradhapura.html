Anuradhapura

The Sacred Ancient Kingdom

Anuradhapura is one of Sri Lanka's most important historic capitals, known for giant stupas, monastic ruins, and sacred Buddhist heritage sites that have stood for over two thousand years.

Highlights & Experiences

  • Ruwanwelisaya: One of the largest and most revered stupas in Sri Lanka.
  • Sri Maha Bodhi: Sacred tree grown from a branch of the original Bodhi tree.
  • Ancient Reservoirs: Ingenious hydraulic engineering from early kingdoms.

Quick Facts for Travelers

  • Best Time: December to March.
  • Tip: Dress modestly when visiting sacred sites.
  • Main Highlight: UNESCO listed sacred city complex.
