Set sail from Mirissa harbour to witness blue whales — the largest animals ever to have lived on Earth — alongside sperm whales, spinner dolphins, and flying fish off Sri Lanka's southern coast.

Plan Your Visit
Key tips for a rewarding and responsible whale watching experience off Mirissa's southern coast.
Best Season
November to April is peak whale watching season on the south coast. Blue whales are most frequently spotted December through March when seas are calmer.
Responsible Watching
Choose a licensed operator that follows marine wildlife guidelines — keep a safe distance, no chasing, no sudden engine revs near whales. Avoid operators with poor reviews.
Travel Tips
Boats depart around 6–7 AM and return by midday. Take sea sickness tablets the night before if needed. Bring sunscreen, a hat, and a light waterproof layer for spray on the open ocean.
