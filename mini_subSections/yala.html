Yala National Park

Yala National Park is Sri Lanka’s most famous wildlife reserve, known for having one of the highest leopard densities in the world. Its diverse landscapes include dry forests, grasslands, lagoons, and coastal areas along the Indian Ocean.

Top Highlights

  • Leopards: Yala offers one of the best chances in the world to spot wild leopards.
  • Elephants: Large herds are frequently seen roaming the park.
  • Birdlife: Home to hundreds of bird species, including migratory birds.

Traveler Tips

  • Best Time: Early morning or late afternoon safaris for wildlife sightings.
  • Safari Jeeps: Book with licensed guides for the best experience.
  • What to Bring: Sun protection, binoculars, and water.
