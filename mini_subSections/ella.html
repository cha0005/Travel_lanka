Ella

Nestled in Sri Lanka’s hill country, Ella is a laid-back mountain village surrounded by tea plantations, waterfalls, and mist-covered peaks. It is a favorite stop for hikers, nature lovers, and travelers arriving by the iconic hill-country train.

Top Highlights

  • Nine Arch Bridge: A stunning colonial-era railway bridge set among lush jungle and tea fields.
  • Little Adam’s Peak: An easy hike offering sweeping panoramic views of the valley.
  • Ella Rock: A more challenging trek, best climbed at sunrise for dramatic scenery.

Traveler Tips

  • Best Time: Early mornings are ideal for clear views before clouds roll in.
  • Getting Around: Most attractions are reachable on foot or by tuk-tuk.
  • Weather: Evenings can be cool, so carry a light jacket.
