Polonnaruwa

Polonnaruwa is one of Sri Lanka’s ancient capitals, renowned for its remarkably preserved ruins, grand stone monuments, and sacred Buddhist sites. The city flourished between the 11th and 13th centuries and is now a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Top Highlights

  • Gal Vihara: Magnificent rock-carved Buddha statues considered masterpieces of ancient art.
  • Royal Palace: The remains of King Parakramabahu’s vast palace complex.
  • Parakrama Samudra: A massive ancient reservoir showcasing advanced irrigation engineering.

Traveler Tips

  • Best Time: Early morning or late afternoon to avoid the heat.
  • Getting Around: Renting bicycles is a popular way to explore the ruins.
  • Respect: Dress modestly when visiting religious sites.
