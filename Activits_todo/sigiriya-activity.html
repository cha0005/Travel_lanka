Plan Your Visit
Everything you need for a seamless Sigiriya experience — timing, tips, and responsible tourism advice.
Best Time to Visit
January to April offers the clearest skies and best visibility from the summit. Arrive at sunrise to beat the crowds and the heat.
Responsible Tourism
Stay on marked paths, do not touch the ancient frescoes, and hire a licensed local guide to support the community.
Travel Tips
Wear comfortable shoes, bring plenty of water, and book tickets in advance. Combine with Dambulla Cave Temple on the same day.
