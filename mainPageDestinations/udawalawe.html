Udawalawe National Park

Elephant Kingdom of Sri Lanka

Udawalawe is one of the best wildlife parks in Sri Lanka, known for frequent elephant sightings across open grasslands and reservoirs. It is ideal for family-friendly safari experiences.

Highlights & Experiences

  • Jeep Safari: Excellent chances to see wild elephants up close.
  • Birdlife: Rich variety of dry-zone birds and water birds.
  • Elephant Transit Home: Conservation and rehabilitation center nearby.

Quick Facts for Travelers

  • Best Time: Year-round, especially dry months.
  • Tip: Book morning safari for active wildlife viewing.
  • Main Highlight: Reliable elephant encounters in natural habitat.
