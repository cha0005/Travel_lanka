Yala National Park Safari

Track Sri Lanka's legendary leopards at the highest density in the world, witness elephant herds, crocodiles, and sloth bears in one of Asia's most biodiverse national parks.

Explore Yala Wildlife Department
Find Your Vibe
From leopard tracking and elephant encounters to birdwatching lagoons, coastal safaris, and nearby cultural stops — Yala offers a full southern Sri Lanka experience.
Leopard & Wildlife Safari
Yala has the world's highest density of wild leopards — expert trackers give you the best chance of a sighting.
  • Highlights:
  • Leopard Tracking — Block 1

Plan Your Visit
Everything you need for a safe, rewarding, and responsible Yala safari experience.
Best Time to Visit
February to July is the peak safari season when water levels are low and wildlife concentrates around waterholes. Yala closes for part of September — check ahead before booking.
Responsible Safari
Choose a licensed operator, stay in the jeep at all times, keep voices low, and never feed or approach animals. Leopards are most active in the early morning and late afternoon.
Travel Tips
Book Block 1 for the best leopard sightings. Bring binoculars, sunscreen, and a dust cover for your camera. Stay overnight in Tissamaharama for back-to-back morning and afternoon safaris.
