Kitulgala

The White Water Thrill

Kitulgala is Sri Lanka's top river-adventure destination, known for white-water rafting, rainforest trails, and scenic river landscapes. It is ideal for travelers seeking active nature experiences.

Highlights & Experiences

  • Rafting: Exciting rapids on the Kelani River.
  • Jungle Activities: Trekking, canyoning, and stream exploration.
  • Eco Landscape: Lush rainforest setting with river viewpoints.

Quick Facts for Travelers

  • Best Time: May to December.
  • Tip: Bring a change of clothes for water activities.
  • Main Highlight: Sri Lanka's most popular rafting hub.
