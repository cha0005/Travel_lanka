The Ella Train Ride & Hill Country

Board one of the world's most scenic railway journeys through mist-draped tea plantations, cross the iconic Nine Arch Bridge, and explore the waterfall-laced highlands around Ella.

Plan Your Visit
Everything you need for the Kandy–Ella train ride and a full hill country experience.
Book Your Train Early
The Kandy to Ella train (Class 1 observation car) sells out weeks in advance. Book through Sri Lanka Railways or a licensed agent — don't leave it to the day.
Best Time to Visit
January to April offers the clearest skies. Sit on the right side travelling from Kandy for the best valley views through tea country.
Travel Tips
The Nine Arch Bridge is best photographed around 9–10 AM when trains pass. Bring a light jacket — the hill country gets cool at altitude.
