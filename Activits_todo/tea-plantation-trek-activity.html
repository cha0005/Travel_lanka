Walk through rolling emerald estates at altitude, learn how Ceylon tea goes from leaf to cup, and breathe in the cool mountain air of Nuwara Eliya, Haputale, and the Bogawantalawa Valley.

Plan Your Visit
Everything you need to plan a comfortable and rewarding tea country trek across Sri Lanka's highlands.
Best Time to Visit
January to March is the driest and clearest period for highland trekking. Avoid heavy monsoon months (May–July) when mist can obscure estate views all day.
What to Bring
Nuwara Eliya sits at 1,868 metres — it gets cold, especially at night. Pack a warm layer, waterproof jacket, and sturdy shoes for the muddy estate paths.
Travel Tips
Stop at Labookellie estate en route from Kandy. For Lipton's Seat, arrange a tuk-tuk from Haputale the night before — early starts are essential for clear views.
