Galle Fort

Located on Sri Lanka’s southern coast, Galle Fort is a historic walled city originally built by the Portuguese and later expanded by the Dutch. Today, it stands as a UNESCO World Heritage Site blending colonial architecture, ocean views, and vibrant street life.

Top Highlights

  • The Ramparts: Walk along the fort walls for sweeping views of the Indian Ocean, especially at sunset.
  • Colonial Architecture: Explore churches, museums, and restored Dutch-era buildings.
  • Cafés & Boutiques: Charming streets filled with art galleries, cafés, and local shops.

Traveler Tips

  • Best Time: Late afternoon for cooler weather and sunset views.
  • Getting Around: The fort is best explored on foot.
  • Dress Code: Light clothing and sun protection are recommended.
