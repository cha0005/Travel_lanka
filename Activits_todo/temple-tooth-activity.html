Plan Your Visit
Key tips for a respectful, comfortable, and memorable visit to Sri Lanka's sacred city of Kandy.
Best Time to Visit
January to April brings the clearest weather. Come in July or August to witness the Esala Perahera — one of Asia's most spectacular processions.
Temple Etiquette
Remove shoes before entering, cover shoulders and knees, and keep voices low. Photography is not permitted inside the inner relic chamber.
Travel Tips
Attend the morning or evening Puja for the most atmospheric experience. Combine with Peradeniya Gardens and the scenic train ride from Colombo or up to Ella.
