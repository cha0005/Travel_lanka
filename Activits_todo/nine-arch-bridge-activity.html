Nine Arch Bridge, Ella

Stand before Sri Lanka's most iconic colonial railway viaduct — nine towering stone arches framed by dense jungle, with the Kandy–Ella express rolling through at least three times a day.

Plan Your Visit
Insider tips for getting the best from the Nine Arch Bridge — train timings, photography, and the perfect full-day combo.
Best Photography Times
Arrive between 9–10 AM for the best morning light. The 6:47 AM and 9:45 AM trains from Ella are the most photographed — check Sri Lanka Railways schedules before visiting.
Getting There
A 10-minute tuk-tuk ride from Ella town or a 30-minute walk along the railway track. Take the upper jungle path for the classic full-arch viewpoint shot.
Combine Your Day
Start with a sunrise hike on Little Adam's Peak, visit the bridge mid-morning for the train crossing, then cool off at Ravana Falls in the afternoon.
