Little Adam's Peak, Ella

Hike through misty tea plantations to a panoramic summit above the clouds — sweeping views over Ella Gap, the Nine Arch Bridge, and the southern hill country.

Explore Ella View on Map
Find Your Vibe
Little Adam's Peak is Ella's most accessible sunrise hike — pair it with a waterfall swim, a tea estate walk, or a full day on Ella Rock for the perfect hill country adventure.

Plan Your Visit
Everything you need for a smooth and rewarding Little Adam's Peak experience.
Start Before Sunrise
Leave by 5:30 AM to reach the summit for sunrise. The climb takes 45–60 minutes at a comfortable pace and the early light over Ella Gap is unforgettable.
What to Bring
Wear sturdy shoes — the tea plantation paths can be slippery when wet. Bring at least 1.5 litres of water, a light layer for the cool summit, and a charged phone.
Combine Your Day
Pair the morning hike with a swim at Ravana Falls, lunch in Ella town, and an afternoon at the Nine Arch Bridge for the perfect full Ella day.
