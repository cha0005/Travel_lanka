Plan Your Visit
Key tips for a comfortable and memorable visit to Sri Lanka's ancient medieval capital.
Best Time to Visit
May to September is the dry season and ideal for cycling tours. Start early morning to explore ruins before the midday heat sets in.
Responsible Tourism
Dress modestly at temple sites, stay on designated paths, and hire a licensed guide to protect the ancient ruins and support local livelihoods.
Travel Tips
Rent a bicycle at the entrance for the easiest way to explore. Carry water, sunscreen, and your ticket — the complex is large and the sun is strong.
