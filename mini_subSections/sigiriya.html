Sigiriya Rock Fortress (The Lion Rock)

Built in the 5th century by King Kashyapa, this 200-meter rock citadel once served as a royal palace born out of political conflict. Originally carved as a colossal stone lion, Sigiriya later became a Buddhist monastery and is now a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Top Highlights

  • The Frescoes: 1,500-year-old paintings of the famous “Cloud Maidens,” remarkably vibrant even today.
  • Mirror Wall: A polished wall covered with ancient graffiti left by visitors over 1,000 years ago.
  • Water Gardens: One of the world’s earliest landscaped gardens with a sophisticated hydraulic system.

Traveler Tips

  • Best Time: Arrive before 7:00 AM to avoid crowds and heat.
  • The Climb: Around 1,200 steps, taking roughly 45–60 minutes.
  • The View: Enjoy breathtaking 360° views over forests and farmland.
