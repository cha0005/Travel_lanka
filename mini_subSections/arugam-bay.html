Arugam Bay

Arugam Bay is Sri Lanka’s surf capital, located on the country’s east coast. Known for its relaxed atmosphere, palm-fringed beaches, and consistent waves, it attracts surfers and backpackers from around the world.

Top Highlights

  • World-Class Surf: Famous right-hand point breaks suitable for beginners and professionals.
  • Laid-back Vibe: Beach cafés, yoga spots, and sunset hangouts.
  • Nearby Nature: Close to lagoons, temples, and wildlife reserves.

Traveler Tips

  • Best Time: May to September for ideal surf conditions.
  • Getting Around: Rent a scooter for easy local travel.
  • Evenings: Expect a relaxed nightlife with beachside dining.
