Kandy – Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic

Set in Sri Lanka’s cultural capital, the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic houses Buddhism’s most revered relic — a tooth of Lord Buddha. Located beside Kandy Lake, the temple remains a living center of worship and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Top Highlights

  • The Sacred Relic: A tooth believed to belong to Lord Buddha, enshrined in a golden casket.
  • Daily Rituals: Traditional poojas held three times daily with drums and offerings.
  • Kandy Lake: A peaceful walk surrounding the temple complex.

Traveler Tips

  • Dress Code: Modest clothing required; shoulders and knees must be covered.
  • Best Time: Morning or evening rituals for the best atmosphere.
  • Footwear: Shoes must be removed before entering the temple.
