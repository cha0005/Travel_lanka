Horton Plains & World's End

Highland Plateau Adventure

Horton Plains National Park is a cool-climate plateau with cloud forests, grasslands, and dramatic viewpoints. The trek to World's End is one of Sri Lanka's most scenic highland walks.

Highlights & Experiences

  • World's End: A dramatic cliff-edge viewpoint above the plains.
  • Baker's Falls: Refreshing waterfall along the circular trail.
  • Wildlife: Sambar deer, montane birds, and misty forests.

Quick Facts for Travelers

  • Best Time: January to March.
  • Tip: Start early before mist covers the viewpoints.
  • Main Highlight: Highland trekking with dramatic landscapes.
