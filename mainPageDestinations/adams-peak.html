Adam's Peak (Sri Pada)

A Sacred Mountain Pilgrimage

Adam's Peak is a mountain revered by multiple faiths and loved by trekkers for its magical sunrise views. The night ascent to the summit is one of Sri Lanka's most unforgettable experiences.

Highlights & Experiences

  • Sunrise Climb: Reach the summit before dawn for panoramic views.
  • Pilgrimage Route: Seasonal climb with lit pathways and tea stalls.
  • Spiritual Legacy: Sacred footprint legend shared by many traditions.

Quick Facts for Travelers

  • Best Time: December to May.
  • Tip: Carry a light jacket; summit mornings are cool.
  • Main Highlight: Sunrise from one of Sri Lanka's sacred peaks.
