Jaffna

Jaffna is the cultural heart of northern Sri Lanka, known for its rich Tamil heritage, historic temples, colonial-era landmarks, and distinctive cuisine. The city offers a unique perspective on Sri Lanka’s history, culture, and resilience.

Top Highlights

  • Nallur Kandaswamy Temple: A major Hindu temple and vibrant center of religious life.
  • Jaffna Fort: A historic Dutch-built fort with views over the lagoon.
  • Local Cuisine: Famous for spicy curries, seafood, and traditional Jaffna flavors.

Traveler Tips

  • Best Time: May to September for drier weather.
  • Dress Respectfully: Modest clothing is advised when visiting temples.
  • Explore Nearby: Consider trips to Delft Island and surrounding villages.
